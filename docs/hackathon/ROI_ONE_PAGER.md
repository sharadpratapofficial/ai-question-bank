# ROI One-Pager

## PW Question Bank AI for Coaching Institutes

## 1. Executive summary

PW Question Bank AI reduces the manual academic operations workload required to import questions, verify answer keys, improve solutions, translate content, generate new questions, and quality-check complete test papers.

For coaching institutes handling frequent papers and question-bank updates, the product creates value through both:

- direct time savings
- better paper quality and faster turnaround

## 2. Manual workflow today

Typical manual process for one academic paper or content set:

- type or copy questions from PDFs
- manually map solutions
- review answer keys question by question
- check syllabus spread and difficulty balance
- rewrite low-quality solutions
- prepare translated versions if needed

This usually consumes expert faculty or content-team time across multiple people.

## 3. Product-assisted workflow

With PW Question Bank AI:

- PDFs are imported into structured data faster
- AI verifies answers and solutions
- translation is accelerated
- fresh questions can be generated where content is missing
- complete papers can be QC checked before release
- analytics help identify weak areas in the bank

## 4. Sample ROI assumptions

Example for a medium-sized coaching operation:

- papers or major content sets processed per month: 40
- average manual time saved on PDF import and structuring: 45 minutes per set
- average manual time saved on question verification and solution review: 30 minutes per set
- average manual time saved on paper QC and balancing: 20 minutes per set

Total estimated time saved per set:

- 95 minutes

Monthly time saved:

- 40 x 95 minutes = 3,800 minutes
- 3,800 minutes = about 63.3 hours per month

## 5. Cost estimate

Assume blended academic/content operations cost:

- Rs 500 per hour

Estimated monthly labor savings:

- 63.3 x 500 = Rs 31,650 per month

Assume monthly AI/API and infra usage:

- Rs 6,000 per month

Estimated net monthly value:

- Rs 31,650 - Rs 6,000 = Rs 25,650 per month

## 6. Estimated ROI

Monthly ROI estimate:

- net value / cost
- 25,650 / 6,000 = about 4.27x

That means every Rs 1 spent on running the product can generate about Rs 4.27 in operational value under this scenario.

## 7. Non-financial ROI

The biggest value is not only cost reduction.

Additional gains:

- faster paper turnaround
- fewer answer-key mistakes reaching students
- better syllabus balance in tests
- lower reviewer fatigue
- more vernacular-ready academic content
- reusable institutional content instead of scattered PDFs

## 8. Where the product creates the most value

- institutes preparing many mock tests every week
- teams digitizing legacy question papers
- multilingual academic delivery
- centralized question-bank operations across batches and centers
- final academic review before publishing papers

## 9. Recommended ROI claim for the jury

In internal test scenarios, PW Question Bank AI shows the potential to reduce academic content operations time by more than 60 hours per month for a mid-sized coaching workflow, while also improving review quality through AI-assisted verification and full-paper QC reporting.
