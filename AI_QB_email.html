1. The challenges with our current QBG software

Building a single test paper today is manual, expert-dependent and slow:

  • Test creation is tedious. To assemble one full-syllabus JEE Mains paper, a faculty must create a bucket, open each chapter of the syllabus in separate tabs, and hand-pick questions one by one — hours per paper.
  • It relies on memory of exam patterns. The faculty must remember exactly how many questions of each type a given exam needs (e.g. JEE Advanced 2020 Paper 1 vs Paper 2 differ per year). This is error-prone, and QC faculty can pick the wrong mix.
  • QC is a 3-hour manual grind per paper. Every question is solved by hand to verify the answer key, check repeated options, language, diagrams, duplicates across the paper, and syllabus balance.
  • Video solutions are recorded one question at a time — roughly 3 hours per paper.
  • Modifications and translations are fully manual — ~3 hours to modify a paper, and Hindi/regional versions are redone by hand.
  • Ingesting outside/competition papers is a full DTP day — typing a PDF/Word paper into QBG takes a DTP resource an entire 8-hour day.
  • No memory of a batch’s past papers. QBG can’t track which questions a batch has already been given, so it can’t automatically exclude them from new papers — the same question can reach the same students again unless faculty cross-check every paper by hand.
  • Key question types aren’t filterable. Important formats such as integer and single-digit integer answers have no dedicated filter — faculty must pull all numerical questions and then manually sift out the integer ones, which is slow and error-prone.

Net effect: faculty-heavy, error-prone, and impossible to scale across batches and verticals.

2. How our AI-based Question Bank solves it — and its key features

  • One-to-two-click test generation. The app stores presets for most major exams (pattern, question-type mix, difficulty, syllabus balance), so a complete paper is generated in a couple of clicks — no bucket-building, no tab-by-tab picking, no remembering per-year patterns.
  • Agentic AI QC. Multiple AI agents verify the paper end to end — answer-key correctness, repeated options, language, diagrams, duplicates and syllabus balancing. ~80% of questions are auto-cleared; only the ~20% flagged need a human, taking ~30 min instead of ~3 hours.
  • Automated video solutions. The tool generates narrated video solutions for a whole paper, replacing ~3 hours of manual recording.
  • In-app modification & translation. Questions can be edited in the tool and papers translated into Hindi and other languages, instead of being redone manually.
  • Direct PDF/Word ingestion. Competition papers (questions + solutions) are injected straight into the database. Instead of a full DTP day of typing, a resource just verifies the ingestion in ~1 hour.
  • Built to scale. Model-agnostic (10+ AI providers), role-based access, across JEE/NEET/Foundation verticals and online/offline batches.

5. How the numbers are calculated (methodology)

Every figure below is modelled at full deployment (the tool is used by every relevant resource) and expressed in INR. The whole model rests on three inputs: a working year, a fully-loaded hourly rate per role, and the time saved per task.

Step 1  ·  The rate basis

  • Working year = 240 working days × 8 hrs = 1,920 hours per person per year.
  • Fully-loaded hourly rate = annual CTC (salary + benefits + overhead) ÷ 1,920 hrs. This is what one hour of that person’s time actually costs the company.
Role Annual CTC (₹) ÷ 1,920 hrs Loaded rate (₹/hr)
Subject Head35,00,000→1,823
Content Faculty15,00,000→781
Project Coordinator8,00,000→417
DTP5,00,000→260

Step 2  ·  Benefit A — time saved → cost saved

For each role we estimate the hours saved on a typical working day, annualise over 240 days, and multiply by the loaded rate:

Annual ₹ saved = Head-count × Hours saved / day × 240 days × Loaded rate (₹/hr)

What drives “hours saved / day” — the manual → AI basis:

  • QC: ~3 hrs/paper → ~30 min/paper (AI clears ~80% of questions; a Subject Head runs several papers a day → ~6.4 hrs/day recovered).
  • Video solutions: 1 day (8 hrs) per 100 questions → ~30 min per 100 questions.
  • Typing / ingestion: ~6 hrs/paper of DTP typing → ~2 min/paper via bulk PDF/Word extraction.
  • Coordination: ~3 hrs/day of manual chasing & assembly removed by automation.

Step 3  ·  Benefit B — new revenue (margin only)

Licensing the QC platform to external coaching institutes: 1,000 institutes × 50 papers/yr × ₹500/paper = ₹2.50 Cr gross revenue. We count only the 80% margin = ₹2.00 Cr as the benefit (so it is comparable to a cost saving, and nothing is double-counted with time saved).

Total annual benefit = ₹22.64 Cr (time saved) + ₹2.00 Cr (revenue margin) = ₹24.64 Cr
